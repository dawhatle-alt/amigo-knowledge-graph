# AMIGO Starter case procedure

TSA-side case handling steps, in order.

| # | Action | Actionable by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Download the latest AMIGO Starter checklist for Control-M/Enterprise Manager / Control-M Server. | TSA |
| 2 | Send AMIGO Starter canned email with the AMIGO article link with AMIGO Spreadsheet and the AMIGO Starter Checklist. | TSA |
| 3 | Advise customer to complete the questions under the relevant AMIGO Starter sheets of the AMIGO Starter Checklist. | TSA |
| 4 | Customer to provide the answers for the questions under the "Customer" related column. | Customer |
| 5 | Customer to send the completed AMIGO Starter Checklist and AMIGO Spreadsheet back to the case. | Customer |
| 6 | TSA to review the AMIGO Starter Checklist and arrange a AMIGO Discussion with the customer. | TSA |
| 7 | Customer and TSA to complete the AMIGO Starter Checklist during the AMIGO Discussion. | Customer + TSA |
| 8 | Attach the completed AMIGO Starter Checklist to the AMIGO case. | TSA |
| 9 | Add "AMIGO Starter" Keyword to the Subject and Memo field of the AMIGO Starter Case. | TSA |
| 10 | Set the LP and DR field of the AMIGO Starter Case to the correct Control-M Product. | Customer / TSA |
| 11 | Set Version field of the AMIGO  Starter Case to the the version that Control-M will be upgraded / migrated to. | Customer / TSA |
| 12 | Follow with the customer as per AMIGO Starter procedure. | TSA |
